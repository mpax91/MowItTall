Westchester: where a cup of coffee is $7 and a guy with a mower is a luxury good. Here's what lawn care actually costs around here, and what you'd spend doing it yourself. Bring a calculator. Or a stiff drink.

## The Quick Comparison (Quarter-Acre Lot, Per Year)

| | Hire it out | Do it yourself |
|---|---|---|
| Mowing (about 28 weeks) | $1,300 to $1,900 | $40 to $75 in gas, oil, blade, plug |
| Fertilizer + weed control | $400 to $900 | $150 to $250 in bags |
| Seed for thin spots | $200+ with aeration | $40 to $80 |
| Fall leaf cleanup | $150 to $400+ per visit | Free (mulch them) |
| Soil test | Usually not included | About $20 to $30 |
| **Yearly total** | **About $2,000 to $3,500+** | **About $250 to $450** |
| One-time gear | None | Mower $300 to $700, spreader $50 to $120 |

These are ballpark ranges. Prices change by town, lot size, slope, how fussy the crew is, and whether your yard is "basically a baseball field" or "a cliff with some grass on it." Northern Westchester lots of an acre or more often run **$80 to $120 a cut** just for mowing.

## What You're Actually Paying For

**Mowing** is paying for time. That's a fair trade if you hate it or have a giant yard. [Hire it, but be the boss of it](/blog/should-i-hire-a-lawn-service/): tall cut, clippings left, sharp blades.

**The spray program** is where the money goes sideways. Five to seven visits on one schedule for every yard on the route. You can do the same job with a spreader, three [holiday feedings](/blog/holiday-lawn-fertilizer-schedule/) and a crabgrass preventer for a fraction of it.

**Cleanups** are mostly leaves. If you [mulch them into the lawn](/guides/should-i-mulch-leaves-into-lawn/), that bill shrinks or disappears.

## The DIY Shopping List

For an average lawn (about 5,000 square feet of grass):

- **3 fertilizer bags a year** (Memorial Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day): about $35 to $60 each.
- **1 bag of crabgrass preventer** around Tax Day: about $30 to $50.
- **Grass seed** for fall touch-ups: about $40 to $80.
- **Soil test** every few years: about $20 to $30.
- **Mower upkeep:** gas, oil, a spark plug, a blade sharpening. About $40 to $75.

Don't know your lawn size? [Measure it on the map](/measure/). The [free plan](/plan/) turns that number into how many bags to buy.

## The Time Math

A quarter acre with a push mower is about 35 to 40 minutes a week. That's roughly 17 hours a season, plus maybe 5 to 10 more for feeding, seeding and leaves. Call it **25 hours a year to save $1,500 to $3,000.** That's a better hourly rate than most side hustles. And it [counts as exercise](/blog/mowing-the-lawn-is-a-workout/).

## The Smart Middle

Can't or won't mow? Hire the mowing and do the feeding yourself. That's the best deal in Westchester: you pay for the time-eater and keep the cheap, important part. Your lawn gets a person paying attention instead of a truck on a route.

## The Bottom Line

Full service: a couple thousand a year, easy. DIY: a few hundred plus your Saturdays. Hire what eats your time, do what's cheap. And get three quotes, because "the guy your neighbor uses" is not a pricing strategy.
