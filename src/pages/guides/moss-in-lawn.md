Moss is the lawn's way of telling you grass doesn't like it there. Kill it without fixing the reason and it's back in a year.

## What Moss Is Telling You

- **Too much shade.** The number one cause. Most lawn grass wants at least 4 hours of direct sun.
- **Acidic soil.** Common in the Northeast. A [soil test](/blog/lawn-soil-test/) tells you in one try.
- **Soggy or packed soil.** Low spots that stay wet, or ground that's hard as a sidewalk.
- **Thin, hungry grass.** Moss fills any gap. A lawn that's never fed leaves lots of gaps.
- **Mowing too short.** Short grass is weak grass. [Mow at 3.5 to 4 inches](/guides/how-high-to-mow-grass/).

## How to Get Rid of It

1. **Test the soil.** If pH is under about 6, lime it per the test. If it's fine, skip the lime.
2. **Let in light.** Trim low tree branches. Even a little more sun helps.
3. **Kill and rake (optional).** An iron-based moss killer turns it black in a few days. Or skip the product and just rake it out hard with a metal rake. Moss has tiny roots.
4. **Loosen the soil.** Core aerate packed areas. [Aeration guide](/guides/should-i-aerate-my-lawn/).
5. **Overseed in September** with grass that fits: fine fescue for shade, tall fescue for part sun. [Pick your grass](/lawn-101/#grass).
6. **Feed it in fall** so the new grass fills in thick. [When to fertilize](/guides/when-to-fertilize-lawn/).

## Or Let It Win

If a spot gets under about 4 hours of sun and you've reseeded twice, stop fighting. Moss, mulch or a shade groundcover will look better than thin, sad grass, and you'll never mow it again. On my [weed board](/weeds/#moss) moss gets the "or just let it be" vote for a reason.
