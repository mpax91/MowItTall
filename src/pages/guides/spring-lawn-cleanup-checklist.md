The snow melts, the lawn shows up looking like it just got back from a bachelor party, and every neighbor starts raking at once. Relax. Most of what you see in March fixes itself. Here's what's actually worth doing.

## The Checklist

1. **Stay off it while it's soggy.** If you leave footprints, you're packing the soil. Wait.
2. **Pick up the junk.** Sticks, branches, dog toys, that frisbee from August. Your mower blade will thank you.
3. **Rake lightly.** A leaf rake, not a dethatcher. You're just fluffing matted spots and pulling out dead stuff. Think "brush its hair," not "rip it out."
4. **Check for snow mold.** Gray or pinkish circles of matted grass where snow sat longest. Fluff them up with a rake so they dry out. Most recover on their own. [Snow mold and vole guide](/guides/snow-mold-and-vole-damage/).
5. **Check for vole tunnels.** Winding little trails of chewed grass, about 1 to 2 inches wide. Rake out the dead stuff. The grass grows back from the edges. Seed the bare ones.
6. **Fix plow damage.** Torn-up sod at the end of the driveway? Flip it back, step it down, water it. If it's gone, fill with topsoil and seed.
7. **Flush the salt.** Soak the strips along the driveway, sidewalk and road a few times once it thaws. Water washes the salt down past the roots.
8. **Tune up the mower.** Fresh gas, new spark plug, sharp blade. [Do it yourself in a morning](/blog/lawn-mower-maintenance-diy/).
9. **Crabgrass preventer around Tax Day.** The real trigger is soil hitting about 55°F, or when forsythia blooms. [Timing guide](/guides/crabgrass-preventer-timing/).
10. **Get a soil test** if it's been a few years. [Why and how](/blog/lawn-soil-test/).

## When to Start Mowing

Start when **the grass is growing and hits about 5 inches**, and the ground is firm. In most of the Northeast, that's usually mid-April. Don't mow on a calendar date. Mow on the grass.

**First mow height: 3.5 to 4 inches.** That way you only take off about a third. I know. Everybody wants a "clean-up cut." Don't scalp it. Short grass in April is a welcome mat for crabgrass in July. [How high to mow](/guides/how-high-to-mow-grass/).

Bag the first mow only if it's full of leaves and sticks. Otherwise, mulch it.

## What to Skip in Spring

- **Big early fertilizer.** Your lawn's still living off last fall's feeding. Wait for [Memorial Day](/blog/holiday-lawn-fertilizer-schedule/).
- **Power raking.** Tears up grass that just woke up.
- **Seeding the whole lawn.** Spring seed fights summer heat and crabgrass. Patch bare spots if you need to, with a seed-safe preventer. Save the big job for [late summer](/guides/when-to-overseed-lawn/).
- **Panic.** That brown lawn in March is asleep, not dead. Give it two warm weeks.

## The Bottom Line

Pick up, rake light, fix the weird spots, flush the salt, mow high once it's growing. That's spring. The lawn does the rest.
