Everybody has the same question with a different ending: "How long do I wait to mow after ___?" Here's every answer in one place. Screenshot it. Tape it to the garage wall.

## The Big Table

| After... | Wait this long to mow | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Light rain | A few hours, until it's dry | Wet grass tears and clumps |
| Soaking rain | About a day | Soft soil ruts and packs down |
| Frost | Until it melts (late morning) | Frozen blades snap; you'll see brown footprints |
| Overseeding | When new grass is ~4 inches (3 to 5 weeks) | Baby roots pull out easily |
| New sod | 2 to 3 weeks, once it won't lift when you tug it | It needs to root first |
| Granular fertilizer | Once it's watered in (rain or hose) | So you don't scatter or bag it up |
| Liquid fertilizer | About a day, once it's dry | Let the leaves absorb it |
| Liquid weed killer (spot spray) | 1 to 2 days | It needs time on the leaves |
| Granular weed and feed | 1 to 2 days, and don't water for a day | It has to stick to wet weed leaves |
| Crabgrass preventer | No wait | It works in the soil, not on the leaves |
| Lime | No wait | Same deal |
| Core aeration | No wait | Mow right over the plugs; they break up |
| Heat or drought (brown, not growing) | Until it greens back up | Mowing dormant grass just hurts it |

**The label always wins.** If the bag or bottle says something different, do what it says.

## The Ones People Get Wrong

**Mowing before weed killer, too.** It's not just after. Don't mow for a day or two *before* spraying. Freshly cut weeds have less leaf to soak up the spray. Let them get a little shaggy, spray, wait, then mow.

**Mowing new seed too late.** People get so scared of hurting new grass that it hits 7 inches and flops over. Mow it once it's about 4 inches, sharp blade, high setting, dry soil, top inch only. [Track your seed day by day](/blog/is-my-grass-seed-growing/).

**Mowing the morning after a storm.** The grass looks dry. The soil isn't. If your shoes sink, the mower will too.

## The Reverse: How Long After Mowing Can I...

- **Fertilize:** right away. Mow first, then feed.
- **Spray weeds:** wait 1 to 2 days so there's leaf to hit.
- **Seed:** right away. Mow a bit lower before overseeding so seed reaches the soil.
- **Water:** any time. Early morning is best.

## The Bottom Line

Dry grass, firm ground, read the label. And if you're wondering whether it's too early on a Sunday, [I built a checker for that](/blog/lawn-mowing-etiquette/).
