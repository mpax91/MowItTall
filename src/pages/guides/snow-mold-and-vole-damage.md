The snow melts and your lawn looks like a crime scene. Gray circles. Pink fuzz. Little highways of dead grass going nowhere. Breathe. It's almost always cosmetic, and two of the three fixes are "rake" and "wait."

## Which One Is It?

| What you see | What it is |
|---|---|
| Round matted patches, gray or tan, sometimes with cobweb-like fuzz | Gray snow mold |
| Round patches with a pink or salmon tint at the edges | Pink snow mold |
| Winding flat trails of chewed grass, 1 to 2 inches wide, with tiny droppings | Voles |
| Raised ridges and dirt piles | Moles (different animal, different problem) |
| Brown strip along the road or driveway | Salt |
| Torn sod at the end of the driveway | The plow. Always the plow. |

## Snow Mold: What to Do

It's a fungus that grows under snow sitting on unfrozen ground. Worst where snow piled up deepest and melted last.

1. **Rake the patches lightly** once the ground dries. Fluffing the matted grass lets air and sun dry it out.
2. **Wait two or three warm weeks.** Most of it greens up from the crowns.
3. **Reseed what's still bare** in mid-spring, or wait for late summer if it's just thin. [Bare spot guide](/guides/fix-bare-spots-in-lawn/).

**Skip:** fungicide in spring. It doesn't fix damage that's already done.

## Voles: What to Do

Voles are mouse-sized and hungry. All winter they run tunnels between the snow and the grass, chewing as they go. You find out at the melt.

1. **Rake out the dead grass and droppings** from the trails.
2. **Let the edges grow back over.** Most trails fill in by late spring.
3. **Seed the bare ones** with a little topsoil and seed. Keep it damp.
4. **Check your young trees and shrubs.** Chewed bark at the base is the real damage. Wrap trunks with hardware cloth before next winter.

Vole numbers boom and crash every few years. A bad spring doesn't mean a bad lawn forever.

## How to Make Next Winter Less Ugly

- **Keep mowing until the grass stops growing.** Don't let it go into winter long and floppy. The last mow can be a bit lower, about 2.5 to 3 inches. [When to stop mowing](/guides/when-to-stop-mowing/).
- **Don't leave whole leaves on the lawn.** Mow them in. [Mulching leaves](/guides/should-i-mulch-leaves-into-lawn/).
- **Don't pile all the shoveled snow on one spot.** Spread it around.
- **Trim tall grass and brush along the edges.** Voles like cover.
- **Pull mulch back a few inches from tree trunks.** It's a vole hotel.

## The Bottom Line

Gray circles, pink circles, little trails: rake, wait, reseed what's left. Winter loves drama. Your lawn will be fine by May.
