Clover is the weed with the best PR team in the business. Lucky charms. Bee lawns. Instagram. Meanwhile it's quietly taking over your yard because you haven't fed it since flip phones were cool.

## Why Clover Shows Up

- **Hungry lawn.** Clover makes its own nitrogen. Your grass can't. In a lawn that never gets fed, clover wins every time.
- **Mowing short.** Short grass lets sunlight hit clover and the soil. [Mow at 3.5 to 4 inches](/guides/how-high-to-mow-grass/).
- **Thin grass and packed soil.** Clover creeps into any gap.

## How to Get Rid of It

1. **Feed the grass.** This is the big one. Follow the [holiday schedule](/blog/holiday-lawn-fertilizer-schedule/): Memorial Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day. A fed lawn crowds clover out over a season or two.
2. **Mow tall.** 3.5 to 4 inches. Shade it out.
3. **Spot-spray in fall.** A broadleaf lawn weed killer in October (late September if you aren't seeding, and never on new seed). The ones that hit clover hardest list **triclopyr, dicamba, fluroxypyr or MCPP** on the label. Spray the patches, not the whole lawn.
4. **Mow off the flowers first.** If the clover's blooming, bees are on it. Mow, wait a day, then spray.
5. **Overseed the gaps** in late summer so grass takes the space. [When to overseed](/guides/when-to-overseed-lawn/).

**Don't bother:** crabgrass preventer (doesn't touch clover), and "weed and feed" on a whole lawn to kill a few patches. Spot-spraying is cheaper and you use way less. Kids and dogs? Keep them off until it dries. [Kid and pet safe tips](/blog/kid-and-pet-safe-lawn-care/).

## Or Keep It (I'm Not Judging)

Here's the honest case for clover:

- **It's green in July** when your grass is crispy.
- **Dog pee doesn't faze it.**
- **It feeds the grass next to it** by adding nitrogen to the soil.
- **Bees love it.** Pollinators need all the help they can get.
- **It's soft.** Barefoot-approved (bee check first).

If you like it, there's even **microclover**, a smaller variety people mix into grass seed on purpose. It blends in better and flowers less.

The downside: it stains white pants, it gets slick when wet, and if you want perfect stripes, clover breaks them up. Your call.

## The Bottom Line

Clover means hungry grass. Feed the lawn and mow tall, spot-spray in fall if you want it gone. Or make peace with it. Either way, now you know why it's there.
