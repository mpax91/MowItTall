Overseeding is the single best thing you can do for a thin lawn. It's also the easiest to mess up, mostly by doing it in the wrong month. Here's the whole thing, start to finish.

## August: Get Ready

- **Pick the seed.** Match your sun and your yard, not the picture on the bag. [Pick your grass in 4 taps](/lawn-101/#grass): it tells you the mix and the pounds for your lawn.
- **Measure.** You need square feet to buy the right amount. [Measure on a map](/measure/).
- **Buy:** seed (at the overseeding rate, about half the new-lawn rate), starter fertilizer, and maybe a bag of compost or topsoil for bare spots.
- **Soil test** if you haven't. [Here's how](/blog/lawn-soil-test/).
- **Stop spraying weeds** on anything you'll seed. Most weed killers and crabgrass preventers stop grass seed too. [The wait times](/guides/weed-killer-and-grass-seed/).

## Seed Day (September)

1. **Mow short.** Drop the mower a couple of notches for this one mow and bag the clippings. You want seed hitting soil, not sitting on grass.
2. **Rake hard or aerate.** Scratch up the soil with a stiff rake, or [core aerate](/guides/should-i-aerate-my-lawn/) if the ground is hard.
3. **Spread the seed.** Use a spreader. Do half going one way, half going the other, so you don't get stripes of grass.
4. **Spread starter fertilizer** the same day.
5. **Rake it in lightly,** about a quarter inch, then press it in. Walking on it with flat shoes works. So does a roller.
6. **Bare spots:** sprinkle a thin layer of compost or topsoil over the seed.

## The Next Three Weeks: Water

Keep the top inch of soil damp. Not soaked, just never dry. That usually means a light watering once or twice a day. [The full watering plan](/guides/how-to-water-new-grass-seed/).

Rye sprouts in about a week, tall fescue in one to two, bluegrass can take three or four. [Track it day by day](/resources/can-i-seed-now/#seedwatch) so you don't panic on day six.

## After It Sprouts

- **Water less often, but deeper,** as the grass grows in.
- **First mow** when the new grass reaches about 4 inches, with a sharp blade, at your normal 3.5 inches.
- **Labor Day / Columbus Day feeding:** still on, unless you just put down starter. Don't double up within a couple of weeks.
- **No weed killer on new grass** until it's been mowed three or four times. That usually means next spring.
- **Rake leaves gently** off new grass in October. Or mow them, high, once the new grass has been mowed a few times.

That's it. Next June, you'll forget you ever had bare spots.
