**A good landscaper leaves your grass about 3.5 inches tall, with clean-cut tips, clippings in the lawn (not the street), and no ruts. If you see dirt, shredded brown tips or the same wheel tracks every week, they’re hurting your lawn. Most of it gets fixed with one text.**

You pay this person every week. You’ve never once graded them. Your kid’s teacher gets a report card. Your Uber driver gets stars. Your landscaper gets a check and a wave.

Time to fix that. Go look at your lawn (or think back to summer) and answer honestly.

## How to Read Your Lawn Like a Detective

**The height.** Walk out the day after a mow. If the grass doesn’t cover your shoelaces, it’s too short. Short grass lets in sun, sun wakes up weed seeds, and the lawn fries in August. [3.5 inches](/blog/your-landscaper-is-scalping-your-lawn/) is the goal.

**The tips.** Bend down. Look at the top of a grass blade. Clean, straight cut? Sharp blade. White, frayed, torn like a cheap paper towel? Dull blade. A dull blade rips instead of cuts, and every rip is a little wound. [More on blades](/blog/sharpen-your-mower-blade/).

**The tracks.** Look across the lawn at sunset, when the light is low. See lines in the same spots every week? That’s the crew mowing the exact same route. It packs the soil and leaves ruts. Switching directions fixes it.

**The street.** Clippings in the road wash into the storm drain. It’s messy, it’s lazy, and it’s lawn food going in the trash. Blow it back onto the grass.

**The wet mow.** Mowing a soaking lawn tears the grass, leaves green clumps that smother it, and digs ruts in soft ground. One day of waiting fixes all of that.

**The August mow.** Brown, dormant grass in a dry spell isn’t growing. Mowing it just grinds it into dust and stresses it more. [Here’s whether it’s dead or sleeping](/resources/whats-wrong-with-my-lawn/#dx-summer-brown).

## Before You Fire Anyone

Most crews are good people with bad habits and no time. Nobody told them you care. So:

1. **Send one friendly text** with your top two or three fixes. [I’ll write it for you](/blog/what-to-tell-your-landscaper/).
2. **Give it a month.**
3. **Still nothing?** Ask once more, in writing, and quietly get two other quotes.

And if your grade came back an A, tell them. People never hear it. A good landscaper is like a good mechanic: you hold on tight.
