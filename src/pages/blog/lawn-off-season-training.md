**The grass is asleep. You are not. The off-season is where lawn seasons are won, and the best training is indoors: vacuum stripes in the living room.** Same technique, same satisfaction, and your spouse sees a clean house instead of a man staring at dormant grass.

## Vacuum Stripes: The Drill That Pays Twice

Carpet stripes exactly like grass. The fibers bend one way, the light catches, and suddenly your living room looks like a ballpark.

1. **Start along your longest straight wall.** Your first line sets every line after it. Just like the lawn.
2. **Push out, pull back on the same line.** That’s one light stripe and one dark stripe.
3. **Overlap a little.** Gaps show. They always show.
4. **Finish at the door and back out.** Don’t walk on your work. You wouldn’t drive across a fresh lawn.

**The brownie points:** do it without being asked. Then don’t say anything. Let them find it. A clean house is love. A *striped* clean house is love with craftsmanship.

## Rainy Day? Snow? Mow on Your Phone.

The weather doesn’t get a vote on your training. When it’s pouring, frozen or dark at 4:30, you can still work on your lines.

- **[The quick game on the homepage](/):** line up the mower, hold to mow, beat par. Mind the dog.
- **[The full stripe game](/games/):** eight yards, from a starter home to a ballpark to a snowy Christmas lawn.

Straight lines are a skill. Skills fade. Thirty seconds a day keeps you sharp for April.

## Things the Lawn Wants From You This Winter

Not much, honestly:

- **Stay off frosty grass.** Frozen blades snap, and you’ll get brown footprints in spring.
- **Keep salt off the edges.** Shovel the de-icer onto the driveway, not the lawn.
- **Stake the driveway edges** so the plow knows where the lawn starts.
- **That’s it.** Go inside. Stripe something.

## The Bottom Line

Championships are won in the off-season. Show up in April with sharp blades, a tuned-up mower, a plan, and a spouse who thinks you’re wonderful because the living room has looked amazing all winter.
