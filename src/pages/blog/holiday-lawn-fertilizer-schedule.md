**Labor Day is the one feeding every lawn should get.** Every holiday you add on top makes the lawn thicker and greener. It also makes you mow more. Pick how far you want to go.

Nobody remembers “feed when the soil is between 50 and 65 degrees.” Everybody remembers a long weekend. That’s the whole trick.

|---|---|---|
| Couch Potato | Labor Day | 1 |

| Lawn Psycho | + July 4th (half dose) and Thanksgiving | 5 |

## More Food, More Mowing

Fertilizer is mostly nitrogen, and nitrogen means growth. For a few weeks after every feeding, the grass grows faster. That’s the green you want. It’s also an extra mow some weeks.

So be honest about your Saturdays. One feeding barely changes how often you mow. Five means mowing twice a week in May and June.

Keep each feeding light: about 1 pound of nitrogen per 1,000 square feet, max. Most lawns do great on two or three feedings a year if you leave the clippings. Five only makes sense on a lawn you water. Missed one? Skip it. Never double up the next time.

## The Holidays

**Tax Day: crabgrass preventer.** Not food. It stops crabgrass before it sprouts. The real trigger is soil at about 55°F, usually when the forsythia blooms.

**Memorial Day: a light snack.** Slow-release, go easy. Too much in spring means more mowing and more disease.

**July 4th: Lawn Psychos only.** Half dose, slow-release, only if you water. Never in a heat wave.

**Labor Day: the big one.** Cool-season grass grows most of its roots in fall. Feed them now and the lawn comes back thick in spring. It’s also the best time to seed thin spots.

**Columbus Day: the pre-game feed.** Late October is when our grass looks its best, and the whole neighborhood walks past it on Halloween. That’s why [Halloween is the Super Bowl of lawns](/blog/halloween-is-the-lawn-super-bowl/). Fertilizer takes about two weeks to show, so feed on Columbus Day and the lawn peaks right on time.

**Thanksgiving: the winterizer.** One last feeding after the grass stops growing but is still green, before the ground freezes. It wakes up greener in spring.

## Skip These Two

**Easter.** Too early. The grass grows leaves instead of roots, you mow more, and the soft growth gets disease.

**Halloween.** It’s game day. Fed two weeks ago? You’re set.

## Spreader Rules

- **Look for a 0 in the middle number.** Most lawns don’t need phosphorus unless a soil test says so. [How to read the bag](/blog/how-to-read-a-fertilizer-bag/).
- **Half the setting, two directions.** Same amount, no stripes. [Here’s how](/blog/spreader-settings-half-rate-twice/).
- **Sweep, don’t hose.** Sweep anything on the driveway back onto the grass, and keep it away from streams and ponds.
- **Water it in.** A light rain or short watering helps. A big storm washes it away.
