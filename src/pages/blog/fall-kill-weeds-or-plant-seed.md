**Fall is the best time to kill weeds. Fall is also the best time to plant grass seed. And most weed killers stop grass seed from growing.** So you have to pick, or you have to time it, or you have to use the one trick. Let’s go through all three.

## Why Fall Is Great for Both

**Weeds:** In fall, dandelions and clover start pulling energy down to their roots for winter. Spray them and the weed killer rides along, straight to the root. Spring spraying just burns the top.

**Seed:** Warm soil, cool air, fewer weeds sprouting, and rain coming back. New grass has all fall and all spring to get tough before summer. It’s the best seeding window of the year by a mile.

## The Problem

Weed killers don’t know the difference between a weed seedling and your expensive new grass. Most of them:

- **Stop seeds from sprouting** (crabgrass preventers block all seeds, for months), or
- **Hurt baby grass** (dandelion sprays can damage grass that hasn’t been mowed a few times yet).

Spray and seed the same weekend with the wrong stuff and you’ll get nothing. You will be confused. You will blame the seed. It wasn’t the seed.

## Option 1: Kill First, Then Seed

1. **Third week of August:** spray the weeds.
2. **Wait** about 3 to 4 weeks (or whatever your label says).
3. **Mid-September:** mow low, seed, starter fertilizer, water.

Tight timing, but it works. Miss the window and you’re seeding into October, which is pushing it.

## Option 2: Seed Now, Weeds Later

Seed in September. Leave the weeds alone. Thick new grass crowds a lot of them out by itself. Spray the survivors next spring, once the new grass has been mowed a few times.

Honestly? This is the right call for most people. **Grass first.** Weeds move into empty space. Fill the space and you fix the cause.

## Option 3: The Trick (What I Do)

There’s one weed killer ingredient you can use the same day you seed: **mesotrione**. It stops a lot of weeds from sprouting, including crabgrass, knocks back some young weeds that are already up, and doesn’t stop your grass seed. Most cool-season grasses are fine with it. Bentgrass isn’t.

Two heads-ups:

- Weeds it hits turn **bright white** before they die. Your lawn might look a little bleached for a week or two. That’s it working. Don’t panic.
- It costs more than regular weed killer. In a seeding year, it’s worth it.

For spring seeding, there’s also starter fertilizer mixed with **siduron**, a crabgrass preventer that doesn’t stop grass seed. It won’t touch dandelions, though.

## The Bottom Line

Fall: pick weeds or seed. If you can only do one, **seed**. If you want both, either time it carefully or use the trick. And whatever you use, read the label before you spend $80 on seed.
