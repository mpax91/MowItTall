**Spring seeding works sometimes. Fall seeding works almost every time.** If you can wait until September, wait. If you can’t, here’s how to give it a fighting chance.

Every April, half the street throws down seed. By August, most of it is gone. It’s not the seed. It’s the season.

## Why Spring Seed Loses

**It sprouts with the crabgrass.** Grass seed and crabgrass want about the same soil temperature. They come up together, and crabgrass is a summer weed. It loves July. Your baby grass does not. Kansas State’s turf team calls this out as reason number one spring seedings fail.

**You can’t use a normal crabgrass preventer.** A preventer stops seeds from sprouting. It can’t tell crabgrass seed from the $80 bag you just spread. So you get to pick: stop the crabgrass or grow grass.

**Summer shows up before the roots do.** Fall seed gets two cool seasons, fall and spring, to grow roots before its first summer. Spring seed gets about six weeks. Then it’s 90°F and dry, and shallow roots can’t keep up.

**You’ll be watering in June.** Fall brings cool nights and rain. Spring seed needs babysitting right when the hose bill starts to climb.

Purdue, Kansas State, Tennessee and Illinois extension all land in the same place: spring is possible, fall is better, and if you seed in spring you’ll probably be reseeding in fall anyway.

## If You’re Doing It Anyway

I get it. The dog wrecked a corner, or you just moved in and can’t look at dirt all summer. Here’s how to stack the deck:

- **Go early.** As soon as the soil hits about 50°F, usually April here. Every week earlier is a week more roots before summer. Illinois Extension suggests finishing by mid-April.
- **Use the one preventer that plays nice.** Mesotrione (sold as Tenacity) or siduron can go down at seeding without stopping your grass. Regular preventers can’t. [How weed killers and seed mix](/guides/weed-killer-and-grass-seed/).
- **Pick fast grass.** Perennial rye sprouts in about a week. Bluegrass takes up to a month, which is a month of crabgrass head start. [Pick your grass](/blog/grass-types-northeast/).
- **Small patch? Use sod.** For a bare spot by the driveway, a roll of sod skips the whole fight.
- **No weed killer on the new grass** until it’s been mowed three or four times.
- **Plan to water** through June and July.
- **Expect weeds.** Pull the crabgrass while it’s small. Then overseed the thin parts in September like you were going to anyway.

## The Better Plan

Put crabgrass preventer down on Tax Day like normal. Mow tall all summer. Seed in September, when the soil is warm, the nights are cool, and the crabgrass is dying instead of sprouting. [Here’s how to overseed, step by step](/guides/when-to-overseed-lawn/).
