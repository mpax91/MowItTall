**Most robot mower problems come down to five things: bumpy ground, wet grass, edges, leaves and signal.** Fix those and it mostly just mows. Here's every common headache, ranked by how often it ruins someone's morning.

## 1. "Help, I'm Stuck"

**Why:** low spots, exposed roots, soft wet ground, or a gap between beds that's just narrow enough to trap it.
**Fix:** fill dips with a little soil (topdress and seed in fall), set no-go zones around root zones, and block off the gap it keeps finding. Every lawn has one.

## 2. Ragged Edges

**Why:** the blades sit inside the body. It physically can't cut right up to a fence or bed edge.
**Fix:** a string trimmer every week or two, or a mowing strip of pavers or mulch along beds so there's nothing to trim.

## 3. Ruts and Bald Paths

**Why:** the same route over and over, usually in and out of the charging station, or runs on wet ground.
**Fix:** give the base a firm, wide approach, change the exit points if the app allows, and skip runs after heavy rain. Fill ruts with soil and seed in September.

## 4. Wet Grass Mess

**Why:** mowing soaked grass clumps clippings and tears up soft spots.
**Fix:** use the rain sensor or rain delay, and schedule runs for later in the day when the dew is gone.

## 5. Leaf Season Meltdown

**Why:** heavy leaves clog the deck and smother the grass under it.
**Fix:** pause the robot during big leaf drops. Mulch the leaves with a regular mower ([why you never needed a blower](/blog/you-never-needed-a-leaf-blower/)) or rake them off, then let it back out.

## 6. Boundary Wire Breaks (Wired Models)

**Why:** an edger, an aerator, a shovel, or a critter.
**Fix:** mark the wire's path, tell anyone aerating where it runs, and keep a splice kit in the garage.

## 7. Lost Signal (Wire-Free Models)

**Why:** satellite models need a clear view of the sky. Tall trees and the side of the house can block it.
**Fix:** move the antenna or base higher or further out, and set the worst spots as no-go zones.

## 8. Uncut Tufts and a Shaggy Lawn

**Why:** not enough run time for how fast the grass is growing (May and September especially), or dull blades.
**Fix:** add hours in peak season and swap the blades. ([Settings by season](/guides/robot-mower-settings-by-season/).)

## 9. Theft

**Why:** it's a small, expensive thing sitting alone in your yard.
**Fix:** turn on the PIN and alarm, use location tracking if it has it, and keep the base out of easy view from the street.

## 10. It Ate Something

**Why:** toys, sticks, dog toys, the hose.
**Fix:** a 30-second walk of the lawn before it runs. Run it only when the lawn is empty and in daylight, for the kids, the dog and the wildlife.

None of this is a dealbreaker. It's just the robot version of keeping your blade sharp. And it still mows taller than your landscaper.
