Here’s a fun game. Next July, drive down any street in town and look at the lawns that are brown and crispy. Now look at how short they are.

Same lawns. Every time.

## The crew isn’t evil. They’re in a hurry.

A landscaping crew has 30 yards to hit before lunch. Short grass means a lawn looks "done" for longer, and the mower deck never gets adjusted because nobody has time to adjust a mower deck 30 times a day.

So the deck stays low. Your lawn gets buzzed to about two inches every week. And you pay for it.

## What short grass actually does

- **Sun hits the dirt.** Weed seeds need sunlight to sprout. You just gave them a tanning bed.
- **Roots stay shallow.** Grass roots roughly match the height of the blade. Short top, short roots, brown by August.
- **It needs more water.** Shallow roots can’t reach moisture, so the sprinklers run more and the lawn still looks thirsty.

## The fix takes one sentence

Tell your crew: *"Please mow us at three and a half inches or higher."*

That’s it. That’s the post. If they push back, show them this. If they push back again, find a new crew. There are plenty.

Mowing yourself? Find the lever by the wheel, pull it up to the top notch or one below, and never touch it again until the last mow in November.

**Mow it tall.** It’s on the sign for a reason.
