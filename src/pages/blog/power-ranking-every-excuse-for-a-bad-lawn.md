**Only one excuse for a bad lawn really holds up: shade. The other six go away with free or cheap fixes like mowing taller, leaving the clippings and testing your soil.**

Everybody has an excuse. I’ve heard them all at block parties. Here they are, ranked from least believable to most.

## 7. "I Don’t Have Time."

You have time to watch three episodes of something you don’t even like. Raising your mower deck takes ten seconds. Leaving the clippings on the lawn takes *negative* time. Next.

## 6. "Lawn Stuff Is Too Expensive."

The three biggest upgrades are free: mow taller, leave the clippings, water less often but longer. You can get to "nicest on the street" for the price of two bags of fertilizer a year.

## 5. "My Soil Is Bad."

Maybe! But you don’t know that, because you’ve never tested it. A mail-in soil test costs about as much as a pizza. Until then, this is a vibe, not a fact.

## 4. "It Was Like This When We Bought the House."

So was the kitchen, and you redid that.

## 3. "The Neighbor’s Weeds Blow Into My Yard."

Partially true. But thick grass doesn’t leave room for seeds to land. Your neighbor is the spark. Thin grass is the dry kindling.

## 2. "The Dog."

Respect. Dog spots are real. Water the spot right after, and reseed the worst ones in September. Do not blame the dog for the other 95% of the yard.

## 1. "It’s the Shade."

The only legit one. Grass needs sun, and some yards just don’t get enough. The fix is a shade seed mix heavy on fine fescue, a little patience, and accepting that under that giant oak, moss might win. That’s fine. Moss is green too.
