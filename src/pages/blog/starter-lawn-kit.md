**You need seven things. That's it.** The lawn aisle wants to sell you forty. Here's the starter kit, ranked by how much it actually matters.

## 1. A Mower That Mows Tall

The most important tool you own, and the only spec that matters: it has to cut at **3.5–4 inches.** Mulching is a must. Not sure which kind? [What size mower do I need?](/guides/what-size-lawn-mower-do-i-need/)

## 2. A Broadcast Spreader

Fertilizer, crabgrass preventer, seed and lime all go through it. A walk-behind broadcast spreader (the kind that throws granules in a fan) is forgiving and fast. [How to use it the first time](/blog/how-to-use-a-spreader/).

## 3. A Spare Mower Blade

My secret: own two. Swap in the sharp one in ten minutes and sharpen the dull one whenever. [Why it matters](/blog/sharpen-your-mower-blade/).

## 4. A Rake

A leaf rake for spring cleanup and scratching up bare spots before seeding. A stiff bow rake if you seed a lot.

## 5. A Hose and a Sprinkler

For new seed (non-negotiable) and deep watering in a dry spell. Add a tuna can and you can [measure how much water you're putting down](/guides/how-long-to-run-sprinklers/).

## 6. A Soil Test

Twenty or thirty bucks, once. It tells you what your lawn actually needs, so you stop buying things it doesn't. [How to do it](/blog/lawn-soil-test/).

## 7. A Spray Bottle

A ready-to-spray weed killer for spot-treating the weeds you actually have. Not a whole-lawn sprayer. [Know your weed first](/weeds/).

## Nice to Have

- A **soil thermometer** (or just [check the live one](/resources/lawn-soil-temperature/)).
- A **striping kit,** if you're one of my people.

## Skip

Aerator shoes, mystery "soil activators," a leaf blower, and anything with "12 steps" on the bag. [The full roast](/blog/lawn-stuff-you-dont-need/).
