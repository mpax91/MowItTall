Every lawn guy has a joke problem. You stand in the driveway long enough, admiring your [stripes](/blog/lawn-stripe-patterns/), and a neighbor walks by. You have to say something.

I’ve been saving these up for years. My family has asked me to stop. Instead, I put them on the internet.

They’re ranked. Level 1 is safe for work. Level 5 is the kind of joke where you say it, then go start the mower so you don’t have to see anyone’s face.

## How to Tell a Lawn Dad Joke

- **Timing matters.** Wait until the mower is off. A punchline nobody can hear is just you yelling at a hedge.
- **Commit.** Don’t laugh first. Don’t explain it. Stare at them until they groan.
- **Know your crowd.** The growing season one plays great with lawn guys. Less great with your wife.
- **Never apologize.** You didn’t [bag those clippings](/blog/stop-bagging-your-clippings/). You’re not bagging this joke.

Got a better one? It better be worse.
